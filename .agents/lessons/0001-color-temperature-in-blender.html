Color Temperature in Blender

Lesson 1. Mission: light the Punk Noodles diner interior so it reads as a warm, lived-in room.

1. What the Checkbox Does

In the Light panel, Temperature sets the light's color from a blackbody temperature in Kelvin, "for natural light emission colors" (Blender Manual: Light Objects). Low Kelvin is orange (candle, tungsten bulb). High Kelvin is blue (overcast sky). Your screenshot shows 6500 K, near neutral white, so the diner light reads as plain white.

4. Two More Sliders in Your Screenshot

Exposure multiplies intensity by 2 to the power of the value, so +1 doubles brightness. Normalize keeps total power fixed when the light's size changes (manual).

The manual text fetched for this lesson does not describe Tint. Adjust it and watch the viewport before relying on it.
