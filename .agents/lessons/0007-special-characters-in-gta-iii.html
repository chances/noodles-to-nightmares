Special Characters in GTA III

Mission: load, create, and remove the special characters 1_shady_job uses, and know which GTA III rules differ from Vice City and San Andreas. Target: GTA III, Sanny Builder library gta3.

1. Two Spellings, One Opcode

Both spellings in the project refer to opcode 023C, the GTA III opcode that loads a special character into a slot. The library names it LOAD_SPECIAL_CHARACTER with 2 parameters, a slot and a model name (gta3.json, entry 023C).

SpellingOpcodeArgumentsWhere It Is Documented
Streaming.LoadSpecialCharacter(slot, name)023C2Library, Section 1 of Lesson 4
023C: load_special_actor 'NAME' as 1023C2GTAMods wiki 023C, GTA III syntax
load_special_character 1 1 #SPECIAL01 'FRANKIE'not listed4Project mission files only, not in the library or the wiki
The 4-argument line is in missions/1_shady_job.txt and in the Create Character card. The user reports it is required on GTA III in testing. The library and the wiki both list 023C with two parameters, the form in the table above.

2. The Lifecycle

A special character takes four steps. Each step has its own opcode, and the wiki says the game can crash if you skip the load.

StepLibrary MemberOpcodeJob
LoadStreaming.LoadSpecialCharacter023CRequest the model into a slot
CheckStreaming.HasSpecialCharacterLoaded023DWait until the slot is ready
CreateChar.Create in the project; library name CREATE_CHAR009ASpawn the ped with #SPECIAL01 to #SPECIAL04
UnloadStreaming.UnloadSpecialCharacter0296Free the slot when the ped is no longer needed

The wiki's example sends each special character through the same four steps. It pairs the 0296 unload with 01C2, which releases the ped's references.

3. What Is Specific to GTA III

Most sources you find were written for Vice City or San Andreas, and the numbers differ.

RuleGTA IIIVice CitySan Andreas
Slots for 023C1 to 41 to 211 to 10
Special ped models for 009A#SPECIAL01 to #SPECIAL0401 to 2101 to 10

GTA III also has its own list of 42 valid names for 023C, including FRANKIE, MARIA, and PLAYER. Those names are the options in the project's special character dropdown. The wiki excludes the characters that only appear in cutscenes (GTAMods wiki 023C).

A GTA III Steam mission that loaded EIGHT2 and spawned #SPECIAL01 crashed at 023C. The fix reported on the forum was upgrading scm.ini, so the opcode definitions the editor uses must match the game build (GTAForums thread 946212).

4. Cutscenes

A cutscene object can be a special character. The wiki says 02E5 creates one with a SPECIAL## model, and that model loads through 023C (GTAMods wiki 02E5). Then 02E6 sets the object's animation by name, and the model for that animation must be loaded (GTAMods wiki 02E6).

A cutscene model loaded with 02F3 is a different path. It comes from the HIER section of an IDE file and uses 0248 and 0249 to check and release it (GTAMods wiki 02F3).
