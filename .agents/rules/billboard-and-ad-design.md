# Billboard and In-Universe Ad Design Guidelines

- **Authentic Commercial Presentation**: Advertisements (payday loans, local services, corporate fronts) must look like believable, professional marketing rather than cartoonish scams.
- **No Caricature Mascots**: Avoid greasy "snake oil salesmen" tropes. Use relatable working-class customers, cheerful mascots, or clean product stacks.
- **Typography & Hierarchy**: Use heavy, readable sans-serif typography (Impact/Helvetica Bold style) with strong visual hierarchy and high-contrast color palettes (e.g. bold yellow/red/navy).
- **Direct Call to Action (CTA)**: Always include a prominent, memorable call to action (e.g. phone number, local kiosk/exit direction).
- **Subtle Satire**: Dark humor and GTA satire belong in the copy, predatory numbers (e.g. "400% APR", "No Questions Asked"), and fine-print legal disclosures, not in making the ad look incompetent.
